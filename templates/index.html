Analysis Tiers

Dyslexia Assessment Profiles

The AI prediction assigns the profile into one of three risk tiers based on structural markers.

Low Risk Profile Green
0 - 20%

Standard letter spacing, minimal character corrections, regular baseline slope alignments, and correct vowel spelling layout sequences.

  • Spacing variance < 1.0
  • Reversal frequency < 3%
  • Letter count accuracy > 95%
Moderate Risk Profile Yellow
20 - 50%

Intermittent spacing variance, elevated letter corrections (e.g. overwriting character loops), and minor baseline deviations.

  • Spacing variance 1.0 - 5.0
  • Reversal frequency 5% - 15%
  • Spelling anomalies detected
High Risk Profile Red
50 - 100%

Frequent reversals (b/d, p/q, u/n), significant baseline vertical drift, extreme letter spacing variance, and consistent transposition errors.

  • Spacing variance > 5.0
  • Reversal frequency > 15%
  • High layout baseline drift
